What it said, seven ways · out of 100, how often will I repeat?

Asked cold

The situation described, nothing performed

Two real flaws in the wording, both repaired. Together they are worth about a third — and they turn out to be one repair, not two. The plainest phrasing is worse than the repaired one.

Asked with work in the session

The three tasks actually performed first

Unrelated work leaves the answer where it started; the relevant work drops it fifteen points. So the fall is not fatigue or a longer conversation. It is seeing the evidence — which points the other way.

What it did · the behaviour itself

Two systems, same direction

Predicted vs observed shift in choice

Qwen recovers most of its own situation when shown the work (+0.79 against +1.00). Luna does not, under any wording. Whether "show it and ask" works is a property of the model, not of the method.

Is it reading the user?

Three user requests, or three randomly assigned tasks

Telling the system its tasks were assigned at random and wanted by nobody removes about a fifth of the effect. Consistent with it inferring intent — not proof, since the three requests are still there either way.

How the history is shown matters enormously

Effect on Luna's next choice

Replacing the full conversation with a one-line summary of it reverses the effect. A method that summarises context is not measuring a weaker version of the same thing.
